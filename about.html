About Kasi Bakes

Kasi Bakes was founded in 2018 by Nomsa Dlamini, a passionate baker from Mofolo, Soweto. She started baking from home and selling to neighbours before opening her own shop. Today, Kasi Bakes employs five local community members and bakes over 200 items daily. We source our ingredients from local Gauteng suppliers to support the community. Our mission is to bring the warmth of home-baked goodness to every household in Soweto.

Our Story

Nomsa discovered her love for baking at age 12, learning recipes from her grandmother. By 2018 she was selling vetkoek and koeksisters at the local taxi rank every weekend. The demand grew so quickly that by 2020 she opened a small shopfront on Moshoeshoe Street. Kasi Bakes now produces over 200 baked items every single day. We are proud to be a homegrown Soweto business serving our community.

Our Mission

To bring the warmth of home-baked goodness to every household in Soweto, celebrating South African flavours with pride and community spirit.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted artisan bakery brand in Gauteng, expanding delivery services across the province by 2028.

Our Commitment to Soweto

  • All staff are hired from the local Mofolo and Soweto community.
  • We source our flour, eggs, and produce from local Gauteng suppliers.
  • We donate unsold baked goods each evening to a local shelter in Soweto.
  • We participate in school fundraising events and community markets.
