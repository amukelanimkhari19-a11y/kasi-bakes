About Kasi Bakes

A bakery born in the kasi, built for the community.

Our Story

Kasi Bakes was founded in 2018 by Nomsa Dlamini in her family kitchen on Moshoeshoe Street, Mofolo. What started as baking vetkoek and koeksisters for neighbours soon grew into a full-time passion and community institution.

After receiving consistent demand from local schools, churches, and corporate offices, Nomsa opened the first Kasi Bakes storefront in 2020. In 2023, the Pimville branch was added to serve a wider community across Soweto.

Today, Kasi Bakes employs 12 local staff members, sources flour from a Soweto-based miller, and serves hundreds of customers daily. Every product is made by hand using the same traditional recipes Nomsa learned from her grandmother.

Mission & Vision

Our Journey

  1. 2018

    The Beginning

    Nomsa begins baking from her home kitchen on Moshoeshoe Street, Mofolo. Vetkoek and koeksisters sold to neighbours and at local events.

  2. 2019

    First Corporate Orders

    Kasi Bakes secures its first bulk corporate order — 300 loaves for a local school feeding programme. Word spreads fast.

  3. 2020

    First Storefront Opens

    The Mofolo branch opens to the public. Three staff members are hired from the local community. Daily production begins at 4am.

  4. 2022

    Community Award

    Kasi Bakes receives the Soweto Business Excellence Award for Community Impact, recognising its local employment and sourcing practices.

  5. 2023

    Pimville Branch Opens

    A second branch opens in Pimville to meet growing demand. The team grows to 12 full-time staff members.

  6. 2026

    Online Presence Launched

    Kasi Bakes launches its website, enabling customers across Soweto and Johannesburg to browse products and place enquiries online.
