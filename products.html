Our Products

At Kasi Bakes, we offer a wide range of freshly baked goods made from quality ingredients. Our breads are baked every morning and available until sold out. We also offer traditional South African bakes like vetkoek and koeksisters. Custom cakes are available for birthdays, graduations, and special events. All bulk and custom orders require at least 48 hours advance notice.
