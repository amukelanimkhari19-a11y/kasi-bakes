Place an Enquiry

Use this form to submit a custom or bulk order enquiry to Kasi Bakes. Fill in your details and describe what you need as clearly as possible. We will contact you within 24 hours to confirm your order and discuss pricing. A deposit is required to confirm all custom and bulk orders. Delivery is available for orders over R300 within the Soweto area.

How It Works

  1. Fill in the enquiry form below with your order details.
  2. We will contact you within 24 hours to confirm availability and pricing.
  3. A deposit is required to confirm all custom and bulk orders.
  4. Collection is available from our Mofolo shop or delivery for orders over R300.

Please note: Custom cakes and bulk orders require a minimum of 48 hours advance notice. For large corporate orders please allow 5 business days.
